How this works

A workflow is a list of stages. Starting one opens a case. Each stage is handled by one kind of person, and checks must pass before it can move on.

Who handles a stage

Applicant
The person who started the case, with or without an account.
Workflow users
Staff who hold a role such as officer, manager or admin.
Outside party
Someone with no account, such as a verifier, using a private single-use link.
System
Automatic checks and certificate issuing.

Deadlines

A stage can have a deadline. If it is missed the reason is recorded and notified, and admins can reset the stage, reassign it, extend the deadline or accept the delay.
